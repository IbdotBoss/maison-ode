# DESIGN BRIEF — Maison Ode Perfume Webapp

Page kind:
- App / Landing with interactive cart flow

Audience:
- Affluent fragrance buyers and gift shoppers
- Design-conscious, premium-committed, 28-50

Vibe words:
- Cinematic, atelier, restrained, editorial, intimate
- Luxury but not flashy; trust-first pricing and shipping

Reference signals:
- Le Labo, Byredo, Diptyque as atmosphere reference, not literal catalogue
- Dark cinematic noir treatment

Quiet constraints:
- Mobile-first responsive
- Clear pricing, shipping clarity
- No admin panel
- No em-dash characters in visible text

Design read:
- Emissive editorial experience with commerce actions cleanly separated from atmosphere

Brand tokens:
- Palette ink #060606, ink-soft #111111, bone #e8e0d4, bone-dim #b8ad9a, gold #c9a96e, gold-bright #e3c47d, paper #d8cfbf
- Display Cormorant Garamond, body Inter, mono Geist Mono
- Motion: slow cinematic reveals, reduced-motion safe
