# DESIGN BRIEF — Maison Ode

Page kind: Lookbook / brand experience
Audience: Affluent fragrance buyers and gift shoppers, 28-50, design-conscious, premium-committed
Vibe words: Cinematic, editorial, atelier, restrained, immersive
Reference signals: Dessign aesthetic sites (Silencio, Sennep, Big Five, Forner), Eleken best practices (Ellipsus, Lusion, Mode, Teenage Engineering)
Quiet constraints: Mobile-first responsive, trust-first pricing/shipping, no admin panel, no em-dash characters, no template-y card grids
Three-dial quantification: VARIANCE 8 / MOTION 7 / VISUAL_DENSITY 4
Design read: Immersive editorial experience where motion, typography, and palette shift like a luxury magazine spread

Brand tokens (updated from references):
- Palette: Deep charcoal #0d0c0b, warm bone #e8e0d4, bone-dim #b8ad9a, muted gold #a68a64, accent cream #d4cfc4
- Display: Playfair Display (editorial serif), Body: Inter (clean sans), Mono: JetBrains Mono
- Motion: ScrollTriggered reveals, smooth inertial feel, cursor-reactive accents
- Texture: Subtle grain overlay, atmospheric gradients, no flat solid backgrounds

Anti-slop rules:
- No identical card grids in adjacent sections
- No eyebrow labels on every section
- No decorative dividers everywhere
- Full-bleed backgrounds break up container-width sections
- Typography scale varies dramatically (hero vs section labels)
- At least 4 distinct layout families across the page
